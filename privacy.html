Who we are

AYUVA Longevity Institute (“AYUVA”, “we”) provides preventive and longevity health services under physician governance in New Delhi, India. This policy explains what information we collect through www.theayuva.com and in the course of your care, and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us through the booking form, WhatsApp, email or phone: name, mobile number, email address, age and your preferred date.
  • Health information you share before and during your visit: the pre-arrival questionnaire, consent forms, test results and clinical notes.
  • Website use. This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It uses browser session storage only to remember that you have seen the opening animation.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange your appointment.
  • To deliver your care: physician review, your Healthspan Blueprint, programme planning and retests.
  • To keep records required by law and by clinical governance.

We never sell your data, and we never share it for marketing.

Who we share it with

Health information is shared only with the clinicians involved in your care and, with your consent, with your own doctor or a partner hospital for a second opinion. Laboratories that process your samples receive only what they need to run the tests you have agreed to.

How long we keep it

Clinical records are retained for the period required under Indian law and professional guidelines. Enquiry details that do not lead to an appointment are deleted after [12 months].

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw a consent you have given, by writing to wellness@theayuva.com. We will respond within [30 days]. Where a record must be kept by law, we will tell you.

Security

Records are held under ethics- and privacy-guided data practices, with access limited to the people who need it for your care.
