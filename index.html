06Safety & governance

Every therapy is gated by a physician.

Powerful tools need careful gates. Before any therapy, a physician reviews your history, your results and the checks below. If a check is not met, the therapy waits.

Before HBOT

  • ENT clearance
  • No untreated pneumothorax
  • Blood sugar check if diabetic

Before sauna

  • Blood pressure below 150/90
  • No dehydration
  • Electrolytes normal

Before cold plunge

  • No unstable angina
  • No severe Raynaud’s
  • 10-minute observation after
Before you arrive
A health questionnaire and consent are completed 24–48 hours before your visit.
Pre-existing conditions
Anyone with an existing medical condition receives an opinion from a partner hospital before therapies begin.
Your records
Records are confidential and handled under ethics- and privacy-guided data practices. Your data stays yours; we never sell or share it for marketing.

12FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

All questions →
What is longevity medicine?

Medicine that measures how you are ageing — not only whether you are ill — and acts early to protect muscle, metabolism, heart, brain, hormones and sleep.

Do I need to be unwell to come to AYUVA?

No. Most of our members feel well. They come to find out how they are ageing while there is still time to change it.

Why 30 measures and not 180?

Because every test should change a decision. Thirty chosen measures give clearer answers, lower cost and less anxiety. Deeper tests are added only when a result asks for them.

Who reviews my results?

A physician from our clinical team reviews every result with you and writes your Healthspan Blueprint.

Is this a replacement for my own doctor?

No. We work alongside your doctor and share reports with your consent. Anyone with an existing condition receives a partner-hospital opinion.

Are the therapies safe?

Every therapy is gated by a physician, with specific checks before HBOT, sauna and cold plunge. If a check is not met, the therapy waits.

Will I see results?

We do not promise outcomes. We retest at 90 days and show you, number by number, what has changed.

How is my data protected?

Your records are confidential and handled under ethics- and privacy-guided practices. We never sell or share your data for marketing.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the programme and format. Our team shares them on your first call.
