The AYUVA regenerative stack

Tools, not packages.

Each therapy is chosen from your Healthspan Blueprint, cleared by a physician and re-measured. If a therapy will not move one of your numbers, we will not recommend it.

NAD+ & IV

Physician-dosed intravenous nutrients, given with blood-pressure monitoring.

Chosen when your Blueprint shows a nutrient or energy need.

Hyperbaric oxygen

Breathing oxygen in a pressurised chamber at 1.5–2.0 ATA.

ENT clearance first.

Thermal contrast

Infrared sauna followed by cold plunge, in measured cycles.

Regenerative consult

PRP and peptide options discussed only when an indication exists.

Investigational uses are explained as investigational.

AYUVA Sculpt

EMS, HIFEM and radiofrequency for muscle tone and pelvic-floor strength.

Neuro & HRV

Heart-rate-variability biofeedback, red-light sessions and cognitive training.

Guided by your HRV, sleep and cognitive scores.

Yoga & breath

Pranayama, meditation, Yoga Nidra and Dinacharya — part of every programme.

BioReboot nutrition

Anti-inflammatory, low-glycaemic menus planned from your metabolic results.

Safety & governance

Every therapy is gated by a physician.

Powerful tools need careful gates. If a check is not met, the therapy waits.

How we keep care safe

Before HBOT

  • ENT clearance
  • No untreated pneumothorax
  • Blood sugar check if diabetic

Before sauna

  • Blood pressure below 150/90
  • No dehydration
  • Electrolytes normal

Before cold plunge

  • No unstable angina
  • No severe Raynaud’s
  • 10-minute observation after
